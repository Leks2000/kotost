Subscription and Purchase Terms

The Kotost Tarot app (tarot and horoscope). Version of November 13, 2026.

In short. The app is free and has no ads. Kotost+ removes the question limit and unlocks in-depth readings. The subscription renews automatically, and you can cancel it in the store at any time. There is no free trial.

1. What is free

Every day: card of the day, horoscope by sign, the Moon, one question to the cards with an AI reading. All 78 cards, the card guide, the reading journal for the last 7 days, the birth chart, destiny matrix and compatibility by signs.

2. Kotost+

  • Questions to the cards without the daily limit (with a reasonable cap against abuse).
  • Personal horoscope for every day, the month and the year.
  • Full birth chart reading and PDF.
  • Synastry and the full destiny matrix.
  • Your full reading history in the journal.

The subscription is monthly or yearly. The price is shown by the store on the payment screen before you confirm, and may differ by country.

3. Payment and renewal

  • Payment is handled by the store you installed the app from: RuStore or Google Play. It is charged to the payment method in your store account after you confirm.
  • The subscription renews automatically for the same period until you cancel it. The store charges at the end of the current period (Google Play within 24 hours before it ends).
  • There is no free trial.

4. How to cancel

  • RuStore: RuStore app → Profile → Subscriptions → Kotost+ → Cancel.
  • Google Play: Google Play → profile icon → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → Kotost+ → Cancel.

After you cancel, Kotost+ keeps working until the end of the paid period. Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.

5. One-time purchases

  • 5 questions: five questions to the cards beyond the daily limit. Used one at a time, they do not expire.
  • Full birth chart reading for one set of birth details: yours forever.
  • Year forecast: for the chosen year, yours forever.

6. Refunds and restoring

Refunds are handled by the store under its own rules: in RuStore through RuStore support, in Google Play through the order page or Google Play support. If a purchase did not unlock, tap "Restore purchases" on the Kotost+ screen or write via Profile → "Report a problem".

7. About the readings

Readings are written by AI from the drawn cards and planet positions. They are entertainment and a reason to reflect, not medical, legal or financial advice. Kotost gently declines questions about health, court cases and betting. If an answer feels inappropriate, tap "Report the answer" below it: the report goes to the developer.

8. Changes and contact

If these terms change, the new version will appear on this page with a new date; the store will notify you in advance about subscription price changes. Questions: Profile → "Report a problem" or email dextyprod@mail.ru.
